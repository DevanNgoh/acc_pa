      Need support right now?

      If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (US) or contact local emergency services. For help with alcohol or drug use, SAMHSA’s free 24/7 helpline is 1-800-662-4357 (US). Outside the US, a local helpline or your doctor can point you to support.

      This app is a companion, not treatment. Stopping alcohol or certain medications suddenly can be medically risky, so please talk to a doctor first.
